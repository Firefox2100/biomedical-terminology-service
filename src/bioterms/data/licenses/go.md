# Licence of Gene Ontology data

This project uses the Gene Ontology (GO) and GO-published cross-references provided by the
Gene Ontology Consortium. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Consortium.

## Original licence terms

Gene Ontology Consortium data and data products are licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence. Users redistributing GO data should identify
the Gene Ontology Consortium, retain the copyright and licence notices, link to the source where
practicable, and report the GO release date or version.

## Additional mapping data

The GO–Reactome annotation includes GO's class-level Reactome cross-references under the same
GO licence. It also includes independent GO assignments published by Reactome; those rows remain
identified with Reactome provenance and are covered by the Reactome licence notice bundled with
this service.

The GO–Uberon annotation is published inside Uberon and is covered by the Uberon licence notice.
The GO–UniProt annotation is published inside UniProtKB and is covered by the UniProt licence
notice. These independently published sources are not relicensed as GO data.
